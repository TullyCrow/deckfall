## Sound

The ambience is made from recordings by NASA spacecraft, all in the public domain, taken from Wikimedia Commons
and slowed, trimmed and filtered for the game. NASA does not endorse this game.

| `sting-signal-1.mp3` | [Bizarre Sounds of Saturn's Radio Emissions (PIA07967)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bizarre_Sounds_of_Saturn%E2%80%99s_Radio_Emissions_(PIA07967).wav) | NASA/JPL/University of Iowa |
